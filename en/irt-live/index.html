About IRT

IRT (Informatsiyine Rehionalne Telebachennya) is a public television channel offering independent coverage of news and socially important topics. The channel is oriented towards forming an active civil society.

IRT broadcasts news, analytical programmes, discussions and talk shows featuring experts, civil activists and government representatives. The channel pays special attention to human rights, anti-corruption and decentralisation.
