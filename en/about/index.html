About

What this site is

Ukraine TV brings Ukrainian TV channels that are openly available live into one place: news, movies, series, music, kids', regional and themed channels. Watching is free and no registration is needed.

How it works

The site does not store or distribute video. The player connects to open streams provided by third parties. The same channels are available in the Telegram app @UkraineTVHub_bot.
