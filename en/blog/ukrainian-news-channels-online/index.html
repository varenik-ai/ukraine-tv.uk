Where to watch Ukrainian news online

If you need live news, the "News & general" section groups seven channels. Here is a short overview of what you can find on them.

  • Pershyi — the national channel of the public broadcaster: news bulletins, documentaries, culture.
  • 5 Kanal — a news channel with bulletins and political analysis.
  • Espreso TV — news, interviews and political debates.
  • Kyiv 24 — news from the capital and city topics.
  • 24 Kanal — up-to-the-minute bulletins and reports.
  • Rada TV — the parliamentary channel: broadcasts of Verkhovna Rada sessions.
  • 1+1 Marathon — a round-the-clock news marathon.

Staying on top of events

Bookmark a few channels in your browser and switch between them quickly in the Telegram app. On channel pages with a programme guide, the "Today's schedule" block shows what is on air right now.

About the sources

We only provide access to openly available channel streams; each broadcaster sets its own editorial policy. For a full picture it helps to compare several sources.
